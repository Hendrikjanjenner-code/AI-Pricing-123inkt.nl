Build a production-grade AI Pricing Decision Engine web app for a large multi-category e-commerce company like 123inkt.

The company sells much more than ink and printers: categories include ink/toner, office supplies, paper, packaging materials, cleaning products, canteen supplies, electronics/accessories, furniture, and other B2B/B2C office-related products.

The app must be built as a serious pilot/MVP, not a toy demo.

Tech stack:
- Backend: Python FastAPI
- Frontend: React
- Database: PostgreSQL
- ML/model layer: pandas, numpy, scikit-learn
- Authentication: simple login system
- File import/export: CSV
- Deployment-ready structure for Replit

Core goal:
Create a Pricing Decision Engine that gives daily price recommendations per SKU, explains why, shows expected margin impact, applies safety rules, and exports approved recommendations to CSV for tools like PriceShape.

App concept:
The app should not use one generic pricing model for all products. It must classify each SKU into a pricing strategy type based on category and product behavior.

Pricing strategy types:
1. SUBSTITUTION_PORTFOLIO
   Used for ink/toner or products with strong A-brand/private-label substitution.
2. COMPETITOR_INDEX
   Used for highly competitive products where market price matters.
3. MARGIN_OPTIMIZATION
   Used for products where margin is more important than volume.
4. STOCK_CLEARANCE
   Used for overstock, slow-moving or end-of-life products.
5. BASKET_BUILDER
   Used for products that help increase order value or are commonly bought with other products.
6. VOLUME_DISCOUNT
   Used for paper, packaging and high-volume B2B products.
7. REPEAT_PURCHASE
   Used for recurring products like cleaning, canteen and office supplies.
8. LOW_VELOCITY
   Used for furniture and slower-moving products.

Pricing model:
Implement a modular pricing engine.

Each strategy type should have its own function:

1. SUBSTITUTION_PORTFOLIO:
   - Estimate own-price elasticity
   - Estimate substitution impact between A-brand and private label
   - Optimize total portfolio margin, not just single SKU margin

2. COMPETITOR_INDEX:
   - Compare own price against lowest and average competitor price
   - Recommend price based on target price index
   - Avoid race-to-the-bottom behavior

3. MARGIN_OPTIMIZATION:
   - Estimate demand sensitivity
   - Recommend margin-maximizing price within min/max guardrails

4. STOCK_CLEARANCE:
   - If stock is high and velocity is low, recommend controlled price decrease
   - Use maximum discount limits

5. BASKET_BUILDER:
   - Keep price competitive if the product contributes to basket value
   - Use basket affinity data

6. VOLUME_DISCOUNT:
   - Recommend price tiers or lower price when volume elasticity is strong
   - Useful for paper, packaging and high-volume B2B products

7. REPEAT_PURCHASE:
   - Avoid aggressive price increases that risk recurring customers
   - Optimize for stable margin and retention

8. LOW_VELOCITY:
   - Avoid frequent price changes
   - Prioritize margin, stock age and availability

Safety rules:
Always apply these before showing recommendations:
- no price below cost unless explicitly allowed
- no price below min_price
- no price above max_price
- max daily price increase: 5%
- max daily price decrease: 5%
- no recommendation for out-of-stock SKUs unless strategy is STOCK_CLEARANCE
- flag low-confidence recommendations
- flag products with insufficient data
- flag extreme competitor price differences
- allow manual override
