Sales Data
Sales Data is an attribute accessible only if you integrate your sales data into PriceShape. Sales attributes provide insights into product performance and user behavior.

Page Views Last Week: Number of times the product page was viewed in the past week.

Items Sold Last Week: Number of units sold in the past week.

Unique Sales Last Week: Number of unique sales transactions in the past week.

Items Added to Cart Last Week: Number of times the product was added to a cart.

Conversion Rate: Percentage of page visitors who made a purchase.

Cart Conversion Rate: Percentage of cart additions that resulted in purchases.

Days on Hand: Estimated number of days the current stock will last based on recent sales.

Last Sale Time: Date and time of the most recent sale.

 

Others
The "Others" category contains additional product-related data fields.

New Price Start Date: The date when a new price becomes active.

Suggested New Price At: The date when a suggested new price was generated.

Triggered Exception: shows which pricing rule exception, can't match, or no vendor, the new price was triggered by.

New Price End Date: The date when the current price ends.

Product Title: The product's title or name.

Number of Matches: The number of vendor matches found for the product.

VAT: The product's VAT percentage.

Stock Quantity: The number of units available in stock.

Shipping Price: The product's delivery or shipping cost.

Inventory Values: The total inventory value for the product based on stock and cost.

URL: The product's website or landing page link.

Image URL: The image link used to display the product.

Stock Status: Indicates whether the product is in stock or out of stock.

PriceShape URL: Direct link to the product's page in PriceShape.

Geotarget: The geographic region or market assigned to the product.

Own Custom Attributes (Text or Dates): Any custom text or date-based attributes you have mapped from your input feed.

Vendor attributes
Vendor attributes are the data points PriceShape collects about your vendors.

Domain: The vendor's website domain.

Source: The source from which the product data is collected.

Last Updated At: The date and time when the vendor data was last updated.

Market Position: The vendor's current ranking position within the market.

Name: The vendor's name as displayed in PriceShape.

Previous Price: The vendor's most recent previous price before the latest change.

Price: The vendor's current product price.

Price Position: The vendor's ranking based on price among vendors.

Price excl. VAT: The vendor's price excluding VAT.

Price with Shipping: The total price including shipping costs, if shipping costs are available.

Product Rating: The vendor's product rating or review score, if available.

Price Difference (%): The percentage difference between your product price and the vendor's.

Quantity: The available quantity or stock level from the vendor.

Shipping Cost: The vendor's delivery or shipping fee, if available.

Stock Status: The vendor's stock availability (in stock or out of stock).

Last price update source: Shows where the latest price was updated, either on the product's URL or through Google, based on the most recent "last seen on" time.

Product URL: A priceshape link that redirects to the vendor's product page.

Marketplace "Buy button": Appears only if a marketplace feed is added to your account.
